Astra Project - Oriented Programming

During my first year at ENSEEIHT, I took part in a team project involving nine students as part of an Object-Oriented Programming course. This project allowed me to apply object-oriented programming principles, contribute to the development of a software application, and collaborate with my teammates using Agile methodologies. We developed a computer game in which the player takes on the role of a character stranded on an island after their spaceship crashes. To escape, the player must gather resources, build factories, recruit inhabitants, and progress through the game to rebuild their spaceship. My role involved defining the different resources and factories in the game, as well as specifying their operating constraints. I also contributed to the implementation of several functional classes, helping bring the game's mechanics to life.

E-portfolio website

As part of an assignment in social and human sciences at ENSEEIHT, I had to create a website. I decided to code it entirely using HTML and CSS, rather than relying on a website builder. This project allowed me to understand how web pages are structured and styled, from organizing content to designing the visual layout. I learned the fundamentals of these languages, improved my understanding of website architecture, and developed a sense of usability and design. This experience made me want to deepen my knowledge in web development, a field that combines logic, creativity, and technical precision.

Zeer Pot Project

This is a modelling of a zeer pot we made to illustrate our report

During my two years of preparatory classes, I worked for four months with a team of four people on a thermodynamic project : the zeer pot. It is a refrigerator that keeps food cool through evaporation. It is used in dry and hot developing countries because it works without electricity and is economical. We first studied the theoretical functioning of the zeer pot. Then we set up experiments to optimise its use.

We also made a tutorial to create your own zeer pot with objects that are easy to find. It was also with this zeer pot that we carried out our experiments.
