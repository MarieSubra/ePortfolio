International Mobility

During our engineering degree programme, we are required to spend a semester abroad immersed in the country of our choice. We have the option of doing an internship or taking courses at a university and earning credits.

University of Birmingham

The University of Birmingham particularly appeals to me for its internationally recognised academic excellence and the opportunity to improve my English through full immersion. It offers a wide range of courses, particularly in artificial intelligence and data science, which are fields I am eager to explore in greater depth. Beyond academics, the university welcomes a large international student community, offering an ideal environment for cultural exchange. Its numerous student societies and vibrant cultural scene would allow me to meet people from diverse backgrounds, discover new activities and enjoy a truly enriching university experience.

National Taipei University

National Taiwan University (NTU) in Taiwan remains an interesting option for my international exchange because the university offers many courses in its Computer Science & Information Engineering (CSIE) department, particularly in software engineering, a field that interests me greatly. The specialities Advanced Software Technology, Development of App Program for Mobile Device and Software Engineering. Taiwan would also be a great opportunity to improve my Chinese and English. NTU is renowned for its international outlook and the diversity of its students, which will allow me to discover new cultures and progress in an enriching environment.

AGH University of Kraków

AGH University of Kraków particularly caught my attention because of its programme in spatial data analysis within the Faculty of Space Technologies. I am especially interested in the Downstream specialisation, which focuses on analysing and exploiting data from space missions, as well as the Biomedical specialisation, covering space medicine and life-support technologies. These fields offer an exciting opportunity to discover new applications of data analysis and explore areas of engineering beyond my current specialisation. Beyond the academic programme, AGH welcomes many international students and offers courses in English, providing an international learning environment. Kraków is also known for its lively student life, which would allow me to meet students from different countries, discover a new culture and enjoy a rich intercultural experience.
